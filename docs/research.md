## Frontier mechanism
Risk-controlling prediction sets turn calibration losses into a distribution-free upper risk guarantee under exchangeability. It is a hard release gate. Primary source: Bates et al., Distribution-Free, Risk-Controlling Prediction Sets, JACM 2024 / arXiv:2101.02703.
## Distinctness
This future action-chain control plane is distinct from artifact containment, regional failover, context transport, retry fairness and tool-memory evaluation.
