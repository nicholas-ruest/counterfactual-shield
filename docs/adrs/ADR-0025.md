# ADR-0025: Publication
Status: Accepted
## Context
We need semantic quality with prospective safety, deterministic evidence and explicit authority.
## Viable alternatives
- tests only
- checker only
## Decision
Use checker plus independent review.
## Tradeoffs
release cost.
## Consequences
This boundary participates in the grant decision and fails closed. Downstream contexts receive typed results rather than ambiguous flags.
## Validation
Validate with exact-main CI and readback, bound to the exact candidate commit.


## Detailed rationale
Publication is permitted only after the indexed candidate passes structural and semantic evidence review, exact-head CI, and governed PR merge. Direct pushes to main, draft PRs, force pushes, and claims based on a local branch were rejected. Research and build Gists are distinct artifacts and must be read back under the correct owner.

## Executed boundary and proof
The publication decision is implemented as a typed boundary, not a documentation-only convention. checker receipt, PR head SHA, workflow run, merged main SHA, and both Gist URLs form the publication proof.

## Review trigger
Revisit this decision if its upstream API, trust boundary, workload distribution, or failure semantics change. A revision must preserve fail-closed authorization, deterministic replay, and an ablation that demonstrates what capability the decision contributes.
