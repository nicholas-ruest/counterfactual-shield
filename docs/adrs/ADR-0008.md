# ADR-0008: Risk control
Status: Accepted
## Context
We need unsafe-release bound with prospective safety, deterministic evidence and explicit authority.
## Viable alternatives
- mean
- Bayesian prior
## Decision
Use finite conformal upper loss.
## Tradeoffs
exchangeability assumption.
## Consequences
This boundary participates in the grant decision and fails closed. Downstream contexts receive typed results rather than ambiguous flags.
## Validation
Validate with coverage and fail closed, bound to the exact candidate commit.


## Detailed rationale
Release uses a finite-sample upper loss quantile from frozen calibration losses with explicit alpha and ceiling. Mean score thresholds were rejected because averages hide tail failures, and an uncalibrated model score was rejected because it carries no coverage interpretation. Invalid, undersized, non-finite, or out-of-range calibration data fails closed.

## Executed boundary and proof
The risk control decision is implemented as a typed boundary, not a documentation-only convention. cs-risk-control high-risk test and NoRisk ablation.

## Review trigger
Revisit this decision if its upstream API, trust boundary, workload distribution, or failure semantics change. A revision must preserve fail-closed authorization, deterministic replay, and an ablation that demonstrates what capability the decision contributes.
