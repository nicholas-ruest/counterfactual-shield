# DDD-0009: Evidence
## Bounded context
Owns DecisionRecord and EvidenceSeal.
## Ubiquitous language
Uses canonical terms and no ambiguous safety booleans.
## Aggregates, entities, and value objects
DecisionRecord and EvidenceSeal. IDs and scores are immutable; lifecycle belongs to the aggregate root.
## Invariants
append-only, linked and sealed.
## Domain events
Publishes EvidenceSealed or VerificationFailed.
## Context map
Facts cross owned ports; infrastructure adapters translate Cedar, RuVector and RVF.
## Ports and adapters
Infrastructure cannot gain authority beyond the typed result.
## Acceptance
Unit invariants plus a real orchestration or integration caller exercise this context.
