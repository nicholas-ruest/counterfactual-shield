# DDD-0009: Evidence
## Bounded context
Owns DecisionRecord and EvidenceSeal.
## Ubiquitous language
Uses canonical terms and no ambiguous safety booleans.
## Aggregates, entities, and value objects
DecisionRecord and EvidenceSeal. IDs and scores are immutable; lifecycle belongs to the aggregate root.
## Invariants
append-only, linked and sealed.
## Domain events
Publishes EvidenceSealed or VerificationFailed.
## Context map
Facts cross owned ports; infrastructure adapters translate Cedar, RuVector and RVF.
## Ports and adapters
Infrastructure cannot gain authority beyond the typed result.
## Acceptance
Unit invariants plus a real orchestration or integration caller exercise this context.


## Detailed domain model
EvidenceSeal contains the RVF witness bytes and digest bound to exact batch material and mitigation totals. DecisionRecord links one seal into an append-only sequence. EvidenceSealed, EvidenceRejected, and DecisionAppended distinguish cryptographic validity from persistence continuity; neither is equivalent to release.

## Ownership and collaboration
cs-rvf owns crypto translation, cs-ledger owns continuity, and cs-verifier checks evidence without writing it. The evidence context exports facts through typed values and never leaks infrastructure handles across the context map. Commands are validated by the owning aggregate; downstream contexts consume immutable results. Failures remain explicit and are not converted into permissive booleans.

## Scenario acceptance
The frozen three-action deployment scenario must cross this context through its public API. Its unit invariant and at least one upstream or downstream collaboration are exercised by locked workspace tests; removing the context must create an observable ablation or eliminate a required release condition.
