# DDD-0008: Planning
## Bounded context
Owns MitigationPlan and steps.
## Ubiquitous language
Uses canonical terms and no ambiguous safety booleans.
## Aggregates, entities, and value objects
MitigationPlan and steps. IDs and scores are immutable; lifecycle belongs to the aggregate root.
## Invariants
budget respected; causes referenced.
## Domain events
Publishes PlanReady.
## Context map
Facts cross owned ports; infrastructure adapters translate Cedar, RuVector and RVF.
## Ports and adapters
Infrastructure cannot gain authority beyond the typed result.
## Acceptance
Unit invariants plus a real orchestration or integration caller exercise this context.


## Detailed domain model
MitigationPlan owns ordered MitigationStep values, total spend, and expected reduction. Its invariants are spend not above budget, no zero-value selection, deterministic tie order, and totals equal to contained steps. Planning may propose constraints but cannot mutate the ActionBatch or issue a grant.

## Ownership and collaboration
cs-plan consumes ResponsibilityScore and action costs; cs-rvf binds the resulting totals into evidence. The planning context exports facts through typed values and never leaks infrastructure handles across the context map. Commands are validated by the owning aggregate; downstream contexts consume immutable results. Failures remain explicit and are not converted into permissive booleans.

## Scenario acceptance
The frozen three-action deployment scenario must cross this context through its public API. Its unit invariant and at least one upstream or downstream collaboration are exercised by locked workspace tests; removing the context must create an observable ablation or eliminate a required release condition.
