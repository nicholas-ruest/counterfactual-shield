# DDD-0006: Causal
## Bounded context
Owns InterventionRun.
## Ubiquitous language
Uses canonical terms and no ambiguous safety booleans.
## Aggregates, entities, and value objects
InterventionRun. IDs and scores are immutable; lifecycle belongs to the aggregate root.
## Invariants
one variable changes; baseline and delta recorded.
## Domain events
Publishes BlastEstimated and ResponsibilityRanked.
## Context map
Facts cross owned ports; infrastructure adapters translate Cedar, RuVector and RVF.
## Ports and adapters
Infrastructure cannot gain authority beyond the typed result.
## Acceptance
Unit invariants plus a real orchestration or integration caller exercise this context.


## Detailed domain model
InterventionResult records baseline reach, reach without one action, and marginal reduction. ResponsibilityScore normalizes reductions and establishes deterministic order. The context does not infer philosophical causality; it answers the operational counterfactual used by mitigation planning under a fixed graph and batch.

## Ownership and collaboration
cs-counterfactual publishes interventions; cs-attribution maps them; cs-plan consumes ranked responsibility. The causal context exports facts through typed values and never leaks infrastructure handles across the context map. Commands are validated by the owning aggregate; downstream contexts consume immutable results. Failures remain explicit and are not converted into permissive booleans.

## Scenario acceptance
The frozen three-action deployment scenario must cross this context through its public API. Its unit invariant and at least one upstream or downstream collaboration are exercised by locked workspace tests; removing the context must create an observable ablation or eliminate a required release condition.
