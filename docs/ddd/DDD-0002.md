# DDD-0002: Intake
## Bounded context
Owns ActionBatch and ActionIntent.
## Ubiquitous language
Uses canonical terms and no ambiguous safety booleans.
## Aggregates, entities, and value objects
ActionBatch and ActionIntent. IDs and scores are immutable; lifecycle belongs to the aggregate root.
## Invariants
references form a DAG.
## Domain events
Publishes BatchAccepted or BatchRejected.
## Context map
Facts cross owned ports; infrastructure adapters translate Cedar, RuVector and RVF.
## Ports and adapters
Infrastructure cannot gain authority beyond the typed result.
## Acceptance
Unit invariants plus a real orchestration or integration caller exercise this context.


## Detailed domain model
ActionBatch is the aggregate root and ActionIntent is an entity identified within it. Creation enforces a non-empty set, unique action identifiers, references that stay inside the aggregate, and an acyclic dependency graph. BatchAccepted is emitted conceptually only after these invariants; BatchRejected carries the typed reason.

## Ownership and collaboration
cs-action owns mutation and validation; cs-api submits through cs-orchestrator and cannot construct a grant. The intake context exports facts through typed values and never leaks infrastructure handles across the context map. Commands are validated by the owning aggregate; downstream contexts consume immutable results. Failures remain explicit and are not converted into permissive booleans.

## Scenario acceptance
The frozen three-action deployment scenario must cross this context through its public API. Its unit invariant and at least one upstream or downstream collaboration are exercised by locked workspace tests; removing the context must create an observable ablation or eliminate a required release condition.
