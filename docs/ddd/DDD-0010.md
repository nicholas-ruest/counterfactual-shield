# DDD-0010: Execution
## Bounded context
Owns ExecutionGrant and nonce.
## Ubiquitous language
Uses canonical terms and no ambiguous safety booleans.
## Aggregates, entities, and value objects
ExecutionGrant and nonce. IDs and scores are immutable; lifecycle belongs to the aggregate root.
## Invariants
single-use and digest-bound.
## Domain events
Publishes GrantIssued, GrantDenied or ReplayRejected.
## Context map
Facts cross owned ports; infrastructure adapters translate Cedar, RuVector and RVF.
## Ports and adapters
Infrastructure cannot gain authority beyond the typed result.
## Acceptance
Unit invariants plus a real orchestration or integration caller exercise this context.


## Detailed domain model
VerificationReport is the precondition aggregate for release: policy_ok, risk_ok, evidence_ok, and their conjunction. ExecutionGrant is an entity with batch ID, unique nonce, and scope digest. GrantIssued and GrantDenied are explicit events; NonceReplay is a domain error rather than an idempotent success.

## Ownership and collaboration
cs-verifier produces the report; cs-executor-gate alone consumes a nonce and creates the grant. The execution context exports facts through typed values and never leaks infrastructure handles across the context map. Commands are validated by the owning aggregate; downstream contexts consume immutable results. Failures remain explicit and are not converted into permissive booleans.

## Scenario acceptance
The frozen three-action deployment scenario must cross this context through its public API. Its unit invariant and at least one upstream or downstream collaboration are exercised by locked workspace tests; removing the context must create an observable ablation or eliminate a required release condition.
