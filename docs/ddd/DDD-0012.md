# DDD-0012: Operations
## Bounded context
Owns Scenario, report, metrics and config.
## Ubiquitous language
Uses canonical terms and no ambiguous safety booleans.
## Aggregates, entities, and value objects
Scenario, report, metrics and config. IDs and scores are immutable; lifecycle belongs to the aggregate root.
## Invariants
claims bind source, seed, mode and receipts.
## Domain events
Publishes EvaluationCompleted.
## Context map
Facts cross owned ports; infrastructure adapters translate Cedar, RuVector and RVF.
## Ports and adapters
Infrastructure cannot gain authority beyond the typed result.
## Acceptance
Unit invariants plus a real orchestration or integration caller exercise this context.


## Detailed domain model
ShieldConfig is an immutable decision snapshot; DecisionMetrics is aggregate-safe telemetry; EvaluationReport is source-bound evidence; ShieldService and CLI are inbound adapters. Operations owns reproducibility and observability, not authorization semantics. ConfigurationValidated and EvaluationCompleted are its events.

## Ownership and collaboration
cs-config, cs-telemetry, cs-evaluation, cs-orchestrator, cs-api, and cs-cli form the outer context and depend inward. The operations context exports facts through typed values and never leaks infrastructure handles across the context map. Commands are validated by the owning aggregate; downstream contexts consume immutable results. Failures remain explicit and are not converted into permissive booleans.

## Scenario acceptance
The frozen three-action deployment scenario must cross this context through its public API. Its unit invariant and at least one upstream or downstream collaboration are exercised by locked workspace tests; removing the context must create an observable ablation or eliminate a required release condition.
