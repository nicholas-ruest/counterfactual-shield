# DDD-0007: Risk
## Bounded context
Owns CalibrationSet and RiskCertificate.
## Ubiquitous language
Uses canonical terms and no ambiguous safety booleans.
## Aggregates, entities, and value objects
CalibrationSet and RiskCertificate. IDs and scores are immutable; lifecycle belongs to the aggregate root.
## Invariants
finite upper bound does not exceed ceiling.
## Domain events
Publishes RiskCertified or RiskRejected.
## Context map
Facts cross owned ports; infrastructure adapters translate Cedar, RuVector and RVF.
## Ports and adapters
Infrastructure cannot gain authority beyond the typed result.
## Acceptance
Unit invariants plus a real orchestration or integration caller exercise this context.


## Detailed domain model
CalibrationSet is the aggregate root for frozen loss evidence. It rejects undersized, non-finite, or out-of-domain samples. RiskCertificate is an immutable value object containing upper loss, alpha, sample count, and pass state. RiskCertified and RiskRejected are the conceptual events consumed by assurance.

## Ownership and collaboration
cs-risk-control is independent of Cedar and RVF; cs-verifier conjunctively combines their typed outputs. The risk context exports facts through typed values and never leaks infrastructure handles across the context map. Commands are validated by the owning aggregate; downstream contexts consume immutable results. Failures remain explicit and are not converted into permissive booleans.

## Scenario acceptance
The frozen three-action deployment scenario must cross this context through its public API. Its unit invariant and at least one upstream or downstream collaboration are exercised by locked workspace tests; removing the context must create an observable ablation or eliminate a required release condition.
